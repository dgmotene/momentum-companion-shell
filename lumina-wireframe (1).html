Explaining your What‑If
I've been playing with the explorer. Why does my premium jump to R1 246 when I move the cover to R650k?
Good question. Two things are moving there. Raising your cover from R500 000 to R650 000 is the bigger part of it, more cover costs more each month. On top of that, you've shortened the waiting period from 12 months to 6, which also adds a little, since you'd have faster access to your benefit after a claim. Together that's the R1 246 you're seeing, about R181 more than now. Just remember it's an estimate, not a final quote.
And if I put the waiting period back to 12?
Then only the cover change would count, so the estimate settles a bit lower than R1 246. The shorter wait is what adds that extra bit on top. Whenever you're happy with a scenario, I can help you set up a proper quote with a consultant.
Ask about any change you’re seeing…
